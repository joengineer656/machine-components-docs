Position and Displacement Analysis

الفكرة: حرّك الذراع المحرّك ثم اعرف أين أصبحت باقي النقاط. الإزاحة تساوي الموضع النهائي ناقص الموضع الابتدائي.

Definitions​

  • Position of a point: vector from origin, e.g. RB=34R_B = 34RB​=34 mm ∠45°.
  • Angular position: angle of a link line from a reference axis (e.g. θ2=134.3°θ_2 = 134.3°θ2​=134.3° ccw).
  • Linear displacement: ΔRC=RC′⊖RCΔR_C = R_C′ ⊖ R_CΔRC​=RC​′⊖RC​ (final minus initial).
  • Rotational displacement: Δθ4=θ4′−θ4Δθ_4 = θ_4′ − θ_4Δθ4​=θ4​′−θ4​ (give sense cw/ccw).

Graphical position analysis​

  1. Draw the mechanism to scale at the initial position.
  2. Move the driver to its new angle.
  3. Respect the joints: arcs of link lengths locate the new pin positions.
  4. Measure the new angles and compute displacements.
الحفّارة: عندما يمتد المكبس الهيدروليكي نريد معرفة أين أصبحت الدلو بالضبط.

Slider-crank (offset L1L_1L1​, crank L2L_2L2​, coupler L3L_3L3​, frame distance L4L_4L4​):

Limiting positions​

Configurations with the follower in an extreme position (crank and coupler lined up, stretched or folded). They give total stroke or throw.

مساحة الزجاج: الوضع الأقصى يمينا والأقصى يسارا، والمسافة بينهما هي زاوية المسح.

Coupler point and full cycle​

Transfer known distances from B and C to locate floating point X′. For a full cycle, position links every 30° or 90° of input and plot output versus input angle — the displacement diagram.

Slider at 155.6 (stroke 80.2 between limits). Limits park the follower — read stroke there, not mid-travel.
