Introduction

Machine Components and Mechanisms — Mechatronics Engineering, Al-Balqa Applied University. Main source: lecture slides (272 slides). Second source: Machines and Mechanisms: Applied Kinematic Analysis, D. Myszka, 3rd ed.

Grading: mid exam 40%, participation 10%, final exam 50%.

How to read these pages​

MarkerMeaning
(no marker)Taken from the slides
Book onlyIn the book but not in the slides
SimulateHard to grasp statically — use the interactive widget on the page
CheckAuthor's own calculation from slide formulas; test yourself against it

Slide notation: +> means vector addition, −> (or ⊖) means vector subtraction. Angles are in degrees.

Coverage: slides cover book chapters 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13–14. Chapters 2 and 8 are skipped, as are outline topics the slides never reach (balancing, joints and joining elements, shafts, keys, bearings, clutches, brakes).
